How to edit
  • Drag an element to move it; the page snaps to its centre and edges.
  • Pull a square handle to resize; the round handle above rotates (hold Shift for 15° steps).
  • Double-click text to jump to its editor.
  • ←↑↓→ nudge, Delete removes, ⌘/Ctrl+Z undoes, Esc deselects.
  • Drop or paste an image anywhere on the window.
